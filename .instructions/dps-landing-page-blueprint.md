**Primary Goal:** Help agents **win more prestige listings** by elevating their offer with branded property showcase websites.

---

## 🎯 Core Positioning (To Guide All Copy & Layout Decisions)

> **Digital Property Showcase (DPS)** is not just a listing tool—it's a **prestige marketing weapon** that agents use to **stand out from competitors** and **win high-value listings**.

## 🖼️ Visual & Design Rules

- Use **polished, high-end imagery**: hero homes, beautiful interiors, elegant branding examples.
- Include **mockups** of a DPS site on tablet/desktop/phone.
- Show **before vs. after** comparisons (portal listing page vs. DPS showcase).
- Keep layout **clean, premium, and uncluttered**. Use plenty of whitespace.
- Stick to **a modern, neutral color palette** with an accent color for CTAs (e.g., navy/white + gold or teal).

---

## 📱 Mobile Optimization Rules

| Rule | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 🎯 CTA visibility | Always keep a CTA button visible (top or sticky footer) |
| 🖼️ Image scaling | Responsive image loading; compress to improve speed |
| 🔡 Font size      | Minimum 16px body, with 1.3–1.5 line height |
| 📏 Button spacing | Ensure tap targets are 44px+ tall with adequate margin |
| ☎️ Call support  | Optional: Add a "Call us to get started" click-to-call link |

---

## ✅ Final Notes

- ✅ **Keep a single focused message:** DPS helps agents win listings by making their marketing unforgettable.  
- ✅ **CTAs must always align with this goal.** No detours into tech jargon or unnecessary detail.
- ✅ **Avoid "justify your fee" messaging.** Instead: _“Raise your value, not your voice.”_
