Frequently Asked Questions

Pricing and Packages

How much does Digital Property Showcase cost?

We offer three tiers: Essential Showcase, Premium Showcase, and Prestige Showcase. For full pricing details, please visit our Pricing section.

Pricing varies by package, with additional setup fees for Premium and Prestige tiers. Discounted rates are available for high-volume agents.

What's included in each pricing tier?

Tiers progressively add more features:

  • Essential Showcase: Unbranded website, custom domain, unlimited photos/videos, 3 months live, 5 website updates, email support
  • Premium Showcase: Adds full agency branding, custom logo, interactive local map, backlinking, 10 website updates, phone support
  • Prestige Showcase: Includes automated Instagram carousel, dynamic open house data, dedicated account manager, unlimited updates, advanced SEO optimization

Is there a minimum contract period?

No long-term contract. Each showcase website is live for 3 months, extendable month-to-month for AU$80 per month.

Showcases provide a unique marketing tool that sets agents apart, demonstrating commitment to innovative marketing and elevating professional image.

Website Customization

Can I customise the website to match my agency's branding?

Yes, Premium and Prestige tiers offer fully branded websites reflecting your agency's unique style, including fonts, colors, logos, and backlinks.

How does the custom branded logo design process work?

We send a Style Selection Proposal with icon, font, and color styles aligned with your agency's brand. You'll choose from logo design examples. This one-time selection guides future logos for your listings.

How does the interactive local map work?

You can nominate up to 10 local landmarks or let us choose based on listing details. The map retains full Google Maps functionality for free exploration.

Content and Support

Is there a limit to photos and videos?

No, you can upload as many photos and videos as needed to showcase the property.

How long does it take to create a showcase website?

Once we have all necessary information, files, and style approval, your showcase will be ready for final approval within 2 business days.

What kind of support do you offer?

We provide email support 24/7 and phone support during business hours. We set up a WhatsApp group for each project to facilitate easy collaboration.

Can I make changes to the website after publication?

Yes, update requests vary by tier: 5 for Essential, 10 for Premium, and unlimited for Prestige.

Marketing and Visibility

Will the showcase website help improve my property's visibility online?

While we optimize for search engines, primary traffic comes from your marketing efforts. The showcase primarily elevates listing and agent perceived value.

This is as much a marketing tool for you and your agency as it is for your listings.

Can I integrate with existing social media marketing?

Yes, we link to your social media pages. The Prestige tier offers an automated Instagram carousel, allowing automatic content updates through social media.
